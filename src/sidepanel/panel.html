What these numbers mean

Est. CTR
The share of searchers we estimate will click a listing where it currently sits. Model output, not measured data. Connect Search Console to see your actual beside it.
Acts like #N
The rank whose normal click-through rate matches what we estimate you will get from where you actually sit. A translation of your position into felt performance — not a second ranking.
Depth, and scrolls
How far down the page a block starts. Your fold reference decides how much fits on one screen, so the same page reads differently on desktop and mobile.
Page height share
How much of the page's total vertical space a block occupies. An AI Overview at 31% is 31% of everything a visitor scrolls past.
