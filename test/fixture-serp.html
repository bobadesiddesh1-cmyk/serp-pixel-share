AI Overview
Personal loan interest rates in India typically range from 10.5% to 24% per annum, depending on credit score, income, employer category and loan tenure. Applicants with a CIBIL score above 750 generally receive the lowest advertised rates. Processing fees usually fall between 0.5% and 2.5% of the sanctioned amount.
Rates are floating or fixed depending on the lender. Most public sector banks publish a benchmark-linked rate, while NBFCs price on internal risk models.
